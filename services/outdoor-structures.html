Walkways & outdoor structures

Custom boardwalks, outdoor staircases, and specialty structures in Central Texas — for slopes, creek banks, and properties that need more than a standard deck.

Some properties don't need a rectangle of deck — they need a boardwalk down to the creek, a staircase that makes a steep slope walkable, or a platform where nothing flat exists. That's some of our favorite work.

Slope builds are their own discipline: footings on grade changes, railings that follow the terrain, and structures that shrug off runoff. We've built winding boardwalks through stands of trees and wide staircases with rustic log railings — pieces that feel like they grew out of the property.

What you get

  • Boardwalks and elevated walkways
  • Outdoor staircases on slopes and banks
  • Rustic log railing and standard railing styles
  • Stock-tank pool decks and specialty platforms
