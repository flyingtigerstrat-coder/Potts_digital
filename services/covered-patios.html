Covered patios

Covered patio construction in Belton, Temple, Killeen, and Salado — solid-roof outdoor rooms with finished ceilings, fans, and lighting.

A covered patio is the difference between outdoor space you use twice a year and outdoor space you use every day. A solid roof means shade in the summer, cover in the rain, and a real outdoor room — with a ceiling fan spinning and a porch swing hanging — instead of a slab that's too hot to stand on.

We build patio covers that look like part of the house, not an afterthought: proper tie-ins to the existing roofline, finished ceilings in stained tongue-and-groove or painted board, and wiring runs for fans and lights planned from the start.

What you get

  • Solid-roof patio covers tied into the existing roof
  • Stained wood or painted ceiling finishes
  • Fan, lighting, and outlet rough-ins
  • Matched shingles and gutters
