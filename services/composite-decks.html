Composite decks

Low-maintenance composite deck construction in Belton, Temple, Killeen, and Salado — hidden fasteners, cable railing, and decking that never needs staining.

Composite decking costs more per board than wood — and then never asks for anything again. No staining, no sealing, no splinters, and the color you pick is the color you keep. For homeowners who want weekends on the deck instead of weekends maintaining it, composite is the answer.

We build composite decks with the details that make them look custom rather than kit-built: hidden fastener systems for a clean surface, picture-frame borders, and railing choices from matching composite to slim black cable rail that disappears in front of a lake view.

What you get

  • Major composite decking brands, installed to manufacturer spec
  • Hidden fasteners for a screw-free walking surface
  • Cable railing and aluminum railing options
  • Framing built for composite's span and ventilation requirements
