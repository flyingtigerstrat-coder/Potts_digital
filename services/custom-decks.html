Custom wood decks

Custom pressure-treated and cedar deck construction in Belton, Temple, Killeen, and Salado — stairs, railings, multi-level decks, and lake-view builds.

A good deck fits the house, the yard, and the way you actually live outside. We design and build custom wood decks — pressure-treated pine and cedar — sized and shaped for your property, whether that's a simple platform off the back door or a multi-level build stepping down a hillside toward the water.

Every deck starts with footings and framing done right, because Central Texas soil and Central Texas summers punish shortcuts. From there it's your call: picture-frame borders, wraparound stairs, bench seating, cable railing to keep a view open, or classic wood balusters.

What you get

  • Pressure-treated pine and cedar construction
  • Multi-level decks, stairs, and slope-side builds
  • Cable railing, wood railing, and custom baluster options
  • Built to code with proper footings, flashing, and hardware
