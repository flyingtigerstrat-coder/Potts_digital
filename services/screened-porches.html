Screened porches

Screened porch and screen enclosure construction in Belton, Temple, Killeen, and Salado — full-height screen walls that keep the breeze and lose the bugs.

Central Texas evenings are perfect — right up until the mosquitoes find you. A screened porch keeps the breeze, the view, and the sunset, and takes the bugs out of the equation entirely.

We build screened enclosures from the ground up and convert existing covered patios into screen rooms: full-height screen panels in wood-trimmed frames, screen doors that latch properly, and tight detailing at every edge, because one gap is all a mosquito needs.

What you get

  • New screened porches and conversions of existing patios
  • Full-height screen walls with wood-trimmed panels
  • Pet-resistant and fine-mesh screen options
  • Screen doors, kick plates, and tight edge sealing
