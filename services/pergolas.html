Pergolas

Custom cedar pergola design and construction in Belton, Temple, Killeen, and Salado — shade structures built for Texas sun and wind.

A pergola turns a bare slab or an open deck into a place you can actually sit at 4pm in August. We build cedar pergolas sized to your space — attached to the house or freestanding — with rafter spacing tuned for the shade you want.

Cedar is the right material for this job: it handles sun and rain, weathers to silver if you let it or holds a stain if you don't, and it's beautiful from the day it goes up. Posts get set right, hardware gets hidden where it can be, and the top stays straight through Texas wind.

What you get

  • Custom cedar pergolas, attached or freestanding
  • Over decks, patios, or standalone in the yard
  • Stained or natural finishes
  • Sized rafters and posts — not undersized kit lumber
