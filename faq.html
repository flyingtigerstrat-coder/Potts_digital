Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to the questions we hear most. Don't see yours? Ask us directly.

Should I build a wood deck or a composite deck?

Wood (pressure-treated pine or cedar) costs less up front but needs staining or sealing every few years. Composite costs more per board but is essentially maintenance-free — no staining, no splinters, and the color lasts. If you plan to stay in the home for years, composite usually wins on total cost of ownership; if up-front budget is the constraint, wood builds a great deck for less.

How long does it take to build a deck?

Most single-level decks take days, not months, once material is on site. Larger projects — multi-level decks, covered patios, screened porches — take longer. When we give you an estimate, we'll give you a realistic timeline for your specific project along with it.

Do I need a permit to build a deck in Central Texas?

It depends on where you live and what you're building — cities like Belton, Temple, and Killeen each have their own rules, and height, size, and attachment to the house all matter. We'll tell you exactly what your project needs during the estimate, and we build to code either way.

How much does a new deck cost?

It depends on size, material, height, and features like railings, stairs, and covers — which is why we do free on-site estimates instead of quoting blind. You'll get a real number for your real project, not a per-square-foot guess that changes later.

Can you add a cover or screen to my existing patio or deck?

Often, yes. Existing slabs can usually take a cover or screen enclosure, and sound existing decks can take a pergola. We'll assess the structure first — and tell you honestly if it can't carry what you want to add.

What areas do you serve?

We serve the Central Texas corridor: Temple, Belton, Killeen, Salado, and surrounding communities. Nearby but not sure? Just ask.
